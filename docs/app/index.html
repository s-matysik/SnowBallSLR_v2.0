SnowBallSLR 2

Citation searching for systematic reviews, in your browser

Backward and forward snowballing that stops on a recorded rule, reports how much it may still be missing, and produces a run archive anyone can verify. Nothing to install. Your screening decisions stay on this machine; the only traffic is queries to OpenAlex and Crossref. Version 1 is described in SoftwareX 36 (2026) 103116; a review started here continues unchanged on the command line.
